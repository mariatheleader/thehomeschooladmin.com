Step 1: Know Maine's Homeschooling Law (It's Simpler Than You Think)

Maine is one of the most homeschool-friendly states in the country. Here's what you legally need to know:

  • You don't need a teacher's certification
  • You don't need state approval
  • You simply notify your superintendent in writing
  • You keep basic records of progress

Maine requires instruction in English, math, science, social studies, PE, health, Maine studies (once), and computer proficiency (once).

This is the foundation of everything. Before curriculum, before co-ops, before routines—you need to know how your child learns.

Step 3: Choose Your Approach (And Why Co-ops Aren't the Answer)

Co-ops are wonderful—but they are not a curriculum. They are community, not structure. They work best after you’ve built a home education system that matches your child’s learning languages.
