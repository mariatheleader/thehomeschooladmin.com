How to Start Homeschooling in Maine (2026 Guide)

1. Learn Your State’s Homeschool Laws

Maine is one of the most homeschool‑friendly states in the country. You don’t need certification, approval, or permission — just a simple written notice to your superintendent and basic record‑keeping.

To get started, review your state’s laws through the Home School Legal Defense Association (HSLDA). Their state‑by‑state breakdown is clear, accurate, and updated regularly.

Start here: HSLDA.org →

2. Position Yourself to Unlearn & Relearn

Homeschooling requires a mindset shift. You’re not recreating school at home — you’re creating a learning environment that actually works for your child.

Before you choose curriculum or routines, give yourself permission to unlearn what school “should” look like. Explore deschooling, unschooling, and gameschooling. These approaches help you reset expectations and build a healthier foundation.

3. Start Building or Finding Your Community

You don’t have to do this alone. Community is one of the strongest predictors of long‑term homeschool success — for both parents and kids.

Start with people who support you, follow your lead, or show interest in what you’re doing. Then expand into local groups, meetups, and enrichment programs.
