How to Start Homeschooling in Maine (2026 Guide)

You’re thinking about homeschooling in Maine, and you’ve probably already started researching curriculum, co‑ops, or online programs. Before you do anything else, this guide walks you through the exact steps to start with confidence.

Step 1: Know Maine’s Homeschooling Law

Maine is one of the most homeschool‑friendly states. Here’s what you need to know:

  • No teacher certification required
  • No state approval required
  • Submit a written notice of intent to your superintendent
  • Keep basic records of progress

Maine requires instruction in English, math, science, social studies, PE, health, Maine studies (once), and computer proficiency (once).

Step 2: Understand Your Child’s Learning Languages

This is the foundation of everything. Learning Languages help you understand how your child:

  • Receives information
  • Processes learning
  • Shows mastery
  • Stays engaged

Once you understand this, curriculum becomes easy — because you’re choosing what fits your child, not what’s popular.

Step 3: Choose Your Approach

Co‑ops, online programs, and group classes are great — but only after you’ve built a home education system that matches your child’s learning languages.

Step 4: Select Curriculum

Now that you understand how your child learns, choosing curriculum becomes simple and intentional.
