That was literacy. There are eight more.

What you just did is a small taste of how I work with families. Literacy is one of the nine growth focuses in my Peace of Mind Framework. Here are the other eight, and the question each one answers:

Social-emotionalIs my child's behavior with siblings and friends normal for their age?
CognitiveHow does my child think, remember, and solve problems?
LanguageIs their speaking and listening on track?
MathAre they behind in math, or just not ready for this curriculum's next lesson?
ScienceHow do I plan science when it feels hard to pin down?
Social studiesWhat does social studies even look like at this age?
Health & physicalAre their motor skills and healthy habits on track?
ArtHow do I make room for art in a way that fits my child?
