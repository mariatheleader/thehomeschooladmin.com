This is the homeschool community families keep asking for again.

Freedom Friends is for Southern Maine homeschool families who want more than random meetups, rushed activities, and one time social events.

This is a consistent Thursday rhythm where children can see the same familiar faces, build real friendships over time, practice communication, grow in confidence, and experience learning through community.

Parents stay present, friendships form naturally, and the group is guided with intention by Maria Galyean, founder of The Homeschool Admin.

The heart of the program

Social emotional learning through real friendship

It may look like children playing, creating, eating, exploring, and talking together. Underneath that, they are practicing the skills homeschool families deeply care about.

Communication

Children practice asking for what they need, listening to others, expressing ideas, navigating group play, and joining conversations.

Teamwork

Activities are designed to help children collaborate, take turns, solve problems, share materials, and contribute to a group rhythm.

Confidence

Shy children are given time to warm up, observe, participate at their own pace, and feel secure in a familiar community.

Leadership

Older children naturally model, mentor, help younger children, and practice the responsibility that comes from mixed age community.

Frequently asked questions

For families looking for homeschool program for their children and community in Southern Maine

Is Freedom Friends a homeschool co-op?

No, parents are not required to plan or teach the children of the group. Freedom Friends is a parent present homeschool community led by The Homeschool Admin. It has the community feel families often want from a co-op, without the responsiblity of having to teach.

Where will gatherings happen?

Gatherings rotate between pre-planned locations including our 'classroom' in Saco, the studio in Scarborough, and other seasonal locations in York and Cumberland county, areas mostly surrounding coastal area. The goal is to keep the rhythm predictable and mapped out clearly for enrolled families.

What ages can join?

The community is mixed age by design. Preschoolers, younger homeschoolers, and older children can gather in a family style environment. Younger children learn through observation and gentle participation. Older children practice leadership, communication, and care for the group.

Is this a drop off program?

No. This is a parent present homeschool community. Parents stay and remain responsible for their children while Maria guides the rhythm, activities, and social emotional learning environment.

Can shy children join?

Yes. This is one of the reasons Freedom Friends exists. The weekly rhythm gives shy children repeated exposure to the same community, which often helps them warm up, build trust, and make friends at a pace that feels safe.

Do preschool families belong here?

Yes. Preschool families receive the same family planning session, Telegram group access, and community support. For younger children, the focus is a gentle beginning, play based learning, confidence, rhythm, and early familiarity with the homeschool lifestyle.

Does this replace my homeschool curriculum?

No, through it does fall into the subject of social studies and Maine studies for homeschool requirements. Freedom Friends supports your homeschool through friendship, SEL, community, shared experiences, and parent guidance. Your family should still choose your core curriculum and home instruction plan, Maria can help you with this in your personalized planning session should your family need suggestions.

What happens after I apply?

Maria reviews your application and sends a deposit invoice if your family is a fit for the current roster. Once your deposit is paid, your family receives Telegram access to schedule your family's personal session, and get first updates for fall, and the summer connection opportunities.

Can I ask questions before committing?

Yes. Parents are welcome and encouraged to email Maria@thehomeschooldadmin.com or message Maria on socials at @thehomeschooladmin with any questions you have before applying.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Freedom Friends is intentionally small and planned around the families who enroll. This policy keeps expectations clear while still honoring real family life.

Enrollment deposits

Enrollment deposits are non refundable and reserve your family’s place for the 2026 to 2027 season. Deposits are applied toward September tuition.

Monthly membership tuition

Monthly tuition reserves your family’s ongoing place in the community. Families may cancel with 30 days written notice.

Missed gatherings

No partial refunds are issued for missed gatherings, vacations, illness, schedule conflicts, or withdrawn participation during an active billing cycle.

Weather and schedule shifts

Gatherings, field trips, or locations may shift for rescheduling due to weather, illness, safety, or venue availability. Updates are shared in the Telegram parent community.
