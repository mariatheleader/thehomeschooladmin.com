This is the homeschool community families keep asking for again.

Freedom Friends is for Southern Maine homeschool families who want more than random meetups, rushed activities, and one time social events.

This is a consistent Thursday rhythm where children can see the same familiar faces, build real friendships over time, practice communication, grow in confidence, and experience learning through community.

Parents stay present, friendships form naturally, and the group is guided with intention by Maria Galyean, founder of The Homeschool Admin.

The heart of the program

Social emotional learning through real friendship

It may look like children playing, creating, eating, exploring, and talking together. Underneath that, they are practicing the skills homeschool families deeply care about.

Communication

Children practice asking for what they need, listening to others, expressing ideas, navigating group play, and joining conversations.

Teamwork

Activities are designed to help children collaborate, take turns, solve problems, share materials, and contribute to a group rhythm.

Confidence

Shy children are given time to warm up, observe, participate at their own pace, and feel secure in a familiar community.

Leadership

Older children naturally model, mentor, help younger children, and practice the responsibility that comes from mixed age community.

Frequently asked questions

For families looking for homeschool community in Southern Maine

What ages is Freedom Friends for?

Freedom Friends is designed for homeschool families with mixed ages, including preschool and elementary ages. The community is intentionally family style rather than strictly age separated so children can learn alongside one another, practice leadership, build confidence, and develop friendships over time.

Is this a drop off program?

No. Freedom Friends is a parent present homeschool community. Parents remain responsible for supervision, bathroom help, snacks, and child safety while participating in the gatherings and outings.

What is the focus of Freedom Friends?

Freedom Friends is rooted in social emotional learning, friendship, confidence, communication, teamwork, leadership, and homeschool community rhythm. While children naturally learn through projects, outings, conversations, and experiences together, the deeper focus is helping children feel connected, confident, and comfortable within a consistent homeschool community.

What is tuition paying for if some locations or outings are free?

Freedom Friends tuition is not simply paying for access to individual locations or standalone events. Some places we visit are free or low cost independently. The value comes from the full structure, facilitation, planning, teaching, leadership, consistency, and community experience built around the year.

Freedom Friends is a continuity, confidence, camaraderie, social emotional learning, and leadership community for homeschoolers. Families are paying for a consistent weekly rhythm, a carefully planned seasonal calendar, coordinated field trips, mixed age facilitation, SEL focused activities, communication, community building, and ongoing parent support.

Many outings are coordinated directly with the organizations so children receive a group educational experience rather than simply attending a public location independently. This includes places like the fire station, animal refuge, veterans home, libraries, museums, educational tours, and local community spaces.

The season also includes projects, student showcases, caroling, leadership opportunities, homeschool picture day with a professional photographer, Maine studies, social studies experiences, friendship building, and opportunities for children to grow more confident socially over time through familiarity and consistency.

Families also receive access to the parent Telegram community along with an included family planning session with Maria The Homeschool Admin for homeschool questions, support, planning, and guidance.

Meals vary throughout the season. Five gatherings include lunch provided by Maria. Some gatherings are potluck style, some are bring your own picnic lunches, and some optional restaurant lunches may happen after field trips for lunch etiquette and community time. Restaurant meals are optional and not included in tuition.

The value for many families comes from not needing to create, organize, coordinate, communicate, and sustain this type of consistent homeschool community experience alone while also homeschooling at home.

What does a typical gathering look like?

Gatherings may include community projects, outdoor experiences, field trips, creative activities, group games, social emotional learning activities, discussions, presentations, free play, leadership opportunities, seasonal celebrations, library experiences, or local educational outings. Some gatherings are more structured while others are intentionally slower and relationship focused.

Will we receive a calendar?

Yes. Enrolled families receive access to the seasonal calendar with planned gathering details, field trips, locations, and important dates. Exact addresses and additional details are shared after enrollment.

Is curriculum included?

Freedom Friends is not a full curriculum program. Families remain responsible for their primary homeschool curriculum at home. Freedom Friends complements homeschool life through community, social emotional learning, leadership experiences, projects, group learning, and parent support.

What homeschool support is included for parents?

Enrollment includes access to the parent Telegram group along with one included family planning session with Maria The Homeschool Admin. Families can use this session for homeschool planning, curriculum guidance, rhythm support, or general homeschool questions within the first three months of enrollment.

How does enrollment work?

Families begin by submitting an application. Approved families receive a $97 enrollment deposit invoice which reserves their family’s place for the season. That deposit is applied toward September tuition.

What is the tuition structure?

New family enrollment is $197 per month for the first participating child plus $50 per month for each additional participating child. Returning families receive priority tuition at $147 per month for the first participating child plus $50 per month for each additional participating child.

What happens if my family needs to withdraw?

The $97 enrollment deposit is non refundable because it reserves a family’s place in the seasonal roster and supports advance planning, scheduling, and preparation. Families may cancel future monthly tuition payments with written notice before the next billing cycle. Partial month refunds are not available once a month has begun.

Can shy children participate?

Absolutely. Freedom Friends is intentionally designed to allow children to warm up slowly over time through familiarity, consistency, and mixed age community. Children are not forced into participation before they feel ready.

Is Freedom Friends a homeschool co-op?

Freedom Friends is a parent present homeschool community led by The Homeschool Admin. It has the community feel families often want from a co-op, with more intentional facilitation, SEL guidance, and homeschool parent support built in.

Do preschool families belong here?

Yes. Preschool families receive the same family planning session, Telegram group access, and community support. For younger children, the focus is a gentle beginning, play based learning, confidence, rhythm, and early familiarity with the homeschool lifestyle.

What happens after I apply?

Maria reviews your application and sends a deposit invoice if your family is a fit for the current roster. Once your deposit is paid, your family receives Telegram access and summer connection opportunities.

Can I ask questions before committing?

Yes. The application is the best place to share questions. Parents are also welcome and encouraged to message Maria on Instagram at @thehomeschooladmin before applying.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Freedom Friends is intentionally small and planned around the families who enroll. This policy keeps expectations clear while still honoring real family life.

Enrollment deposits

Enrollment deposits are non refundable and reserve your family’s place for the 2026 to 2027 season. Deposits are applied toward September tuition.

Monthly membership tuition

Monthly tuition reserves your family’s ongoing place in the community. Families may cancel with 30 days written notice.

Missed gatherings

No partial refunds are issued for missed gatherings, vacations, illness, schedule conflicts, or withdrawn participation during an active billing cycle.

Weather and schedule shifts

Gatherings, field trips, or locations may shift due to weather, illness, safety, or venue availability. Updates are shared in the Telegram parent community.
