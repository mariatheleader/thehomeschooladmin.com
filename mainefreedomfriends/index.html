A NOTE FROM THE HOMESCHOOL ADMIN™

Freedom Friends™

Every season has a purpose.

When I first imagined Freedom Friends™, I envisioned a place where homeschool families could gather each week, build friendships, learn together, and create memories that would last long after childhood.

This season simply wasn't the right season for that vision.

Rather than move forward with something smaller than I believed families deserved, I chose to pause the program and continue building resources that serve homeschool parents wherever they are.

Thank you to every family who reached out, encouraged the vision, shared the program, or imagined your family becoming part of this community.

My mission hasn't changed. I'm still here to help homeschool parents feel confident, organized, and reassured every step of the way.
