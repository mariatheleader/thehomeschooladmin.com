Freedom Friends™

Thank you for your interest in this community and the vision behind it.

Freedom Friends™ will not be moving forward for the Fall 2026 season.

The enrollment needed to create the full community experience was not reached, so I chose to pause the program rather than offer a version that did not match the vision families were invited into.

I am deeply grateful to every parent who asked questions, submitted an application, shared the program, or imagined their family inside this space.

Freedom Friends™ may return in a new season, with a new structure, or in a format that creates even more access for homeschool families.
