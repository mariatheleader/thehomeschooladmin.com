Systems Built

Real Time™ Content Vault / Content Converter

Multi-step AI workflow that transforms voice inputs into structured, ready-to-publish content across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, email, carousels, reels, and calls to action.

  • Telegram-based interface with command-driven workflows.
  • Voice input → transcription → platform-specific copy → organized vault of assets.
  • Demonstrates repeatable output architecture and multi-channel content operations.

Pod BitBot™ (Podcast Automation)

Telegram workflow that builds tagged MP3 files with artist, title, album, track number, year, genre, and comments, returning a creator-ready audio asset.

  • Solves repetitive podcast publishing steps and metadata handling.
  • Shows media asset automation and creator enablement.

The IG Bit Bot

Telegram bot that receives voice notes and lets the user choose an output style: feed caption, reel caption, carousel, story slides, or video-only conversion.

  • Reduces friction between “what I want to say” and “what gets published.”
  • Demonstrates voice-based UX, prompt routing, and sales messaging support.

Instagram → Email Engine

API-integrated workflow that converts existing Instagram content into organized email drafts with reusable templates.

  • Increases leverage from existing content and reduces net-new writing time.
  • Demonstrates automation thinking and system design.

AI Curriculum & Assessment Systems

AI-assisted workflows that transform educational assessment data into reports, recommendations, lesson plans, and resource lists.

  • Shows structured output design and domain-specific enablement.
  • Built on top of qualitative research and real family use cases.
