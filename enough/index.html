Enough
A 3-week homeschool reset

Stop asking the Facebook group whether your kid is "behind." Get a real answer about your real child.

Enough is for the parent who is doing a lot and still lies awake wondering if it's enough. For three weeks, you'll look at your child through the Peace of Mind Framework from my book REASSURED™. That's nine growth focuses, all open to you. I'll tell you what I see and what to do next.

  • Learn the framework together. Short teachings in our Telegram group, so you get the "why" without sitting through a class.
  • Observe your child. Circle where your child is on each growth focus and send me a photo.
  • Get personal feedback for each child. I reply to you one-on-one within 24 hours, with clear next steps.
  • Walk away with a customized lesson plan, so you can see exactly what intentional, child-led planning looks like.

Hi, I'm Maria

After five years with homeschooling families, I've learned most parents don't need more noise. They need calm, specific guidance.

Most homeschool parents can find information. The harder part is knowing what applies to your child, your home, your schedule, and your real life right now.

I hold a Bachelor's in Child & Youth Development and a Master's in Leadership. I've worked alongside 55+ families, preschool through high school, helping them build routines that hold, choose curriculum that fits, create records, plan transcripts, and make decisions without the spiral.

My favorite part? Most families who work with me long-term graduate. After about a year, they don't need me anymore, because they finally trust themselves.
