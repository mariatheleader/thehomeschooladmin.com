Questions

What ages is this for?

Ages 3 through high school. The matrix is personalized to where your child actually is, not just their age. It's especially eye-opening for older kids: the matrix shows both what they've mastered and the specific skills that slipped through along the way, like a teen who reads well but has never learned to write up an article in their own words. A gap that specific is much easier to close than a vague worry about being behind. If you have more than one child, choose one child and one growth focus for these three weeks. You can use the same process with your other kids afterward.

What can I ask you during the three weeks?

Anything homeschool. Curriculum questions, building a schedule that works, behavior that's got you stumped, finding homeschool community near you, or what to do about the subject your kid fights you on. It's your private thread with me for the full three weeks, and I reply within 24 hours.

Do I need to be online at a certain time?

No. There are no live calls. You watch the videos and message me whenever it works for your family, and I reply within 24 hours. No babysitter required.

Is this a group program?

It's both. Everyone moves through the three weeks together, and a private Telegram channel is where I share the teaching videos and instructions. Anything about your own child happens one-on-one in a private chat with me. There's nothing to keep up with and no live calls.

Do I need a printer?

No. You can print the matrix and circle it by hand, or mark it up on your phone or tablet. Either way, just send me a photo or screenshot.

Does Enough cover more than one growth focus?

No. Enough includes one growth focus for one child, so the three weeks stay focused and doable.

If you'd like the full picture afterward, I'm creating a self-guided course called More Than Enough that walks you through every growth focus. Enough families will hear about it first, with a special price, when Enough wraps up.

Do I need to use a certain curriculum?

No. Enough works alongside any curriculum, or none at all. It's about your child, not the program.
