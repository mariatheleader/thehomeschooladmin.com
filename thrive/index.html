Before you read further

Is THRIVE the right fit
for your family?

THRIVE is a private, hands-on year together. It isn't a course, a co-op, or a curriculum box. Here's a quick gut check.

THRIVE is for you if…

  • You considered private school, and you'd rather invest that tuition in an education built around your child, at home
  • You work from home, run a business, or have a demanding career, and you don't have time to figure out each morning what everyone should be doing
  • You love being organized but don't have the patience to build the system yourself
  • You want independent kids who know their own rhythm, with a schedule on the fridge everyone can follow
  • You want a real person who knows your children, all year long

THRIVE is not for you if…

  • You want someone else to be your child's primary teacher
  • You're looking for a curriculum subscription or online school
  • You only need one question answered. Book a Clarity Session instead.
  • You want to understand where your child is right now before committing to more. Start with Enough.
  • You want just a few pieces of THRIVE. It's one complete package, by design.

If the left column read like your life, keep going. This was built for you.

What I do for you

For the next twelve months,
I will…

  • Research and shop curriculum with you, like a personal shopper for your homeschool. No more 40 open tabs, overbuying, or second-guessing at checkout.
  • Build a one-page daily agenda for each child that shows exactly which lesson to do and which materials to pull. Your kids know what's next without asking you.
  • Create custom lessons and activities built around each child's interests and development, so learning feels like theirs and you never scramble on Pinterest at 7am.
  • Track each child's progress with REASSURED™, my developmental framework, so you always know where they are and what comes next.
  • Handle documentation and legal requirements with you, through a done-with-you portfolio and reminders, so year-end isn't a panic.
  • Plan the year ahead and set up a household system a spouse, nanny, or grandparent can follow, so your homeschool still runs on the days you can't.
  • Stay with you all year, through direct chat access and 4 in-depth sessions, in person in Southern Maine or virtual anywhere.

Facebook groups and co-op chats are nice, but they don't build over time. Nobody in them is getting to know your child through a child development specialist's eyes. THRIVE gives you that all year.

How the year works

Four in-depth sessions,
with support in between.

Between sessions, you have direct chat access to me for questions, ideas, wins, and hard days. I reply within 24 hours.

Session 1

Orientation. We observe your days, set each child's REASSURED™ baseline, and build (or rebuild) your daily rhythm together.

Session 2

Household system. We set everything up so a spouse, nanny, or grandparent can run the day without you.

Session 3

Real-life refinement. We adjust agendas, lessons, and materials based on what's actually happening in your home.

Session 4

Year-end and next year. We wrap up documentation and map out the year ahead.

Your pace, your calendar. Most families spread the sessions across the year. If you're brand new or rebuilding from scratch, we can hold them closer together at the start, when you need the most support.

Common questions

Things families ask
before they join.

Why is THRIVE priced like private school?

Because for most THRIVE families, private school is the real alternative. THRIVE replaces the research, planning, and administration a school would do, while your kids stay home with an education built around them. You're not paying for a course. You're paying for a specialist who knows your children, carrying the work with you for a full year.

Is THRIVE open right now?

THRIVE is waitlist only at the moment. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know when spots open. If you need support sooner, Enough and Clarity Sessions are open now.

Can I get just part of THRIVE?

No. THRIVE works because every piece connects: the curriculum feeds the agendas, the agendas feed the tracking, and the tracking feeds the documentation. If you only need part of that, Enough or a Clarity Session is the better fit, and I'm happy to point you to the right one.

We've already started homeschooling. Is it too late?

Not at all. THRIVE can begin at any point in the year. Families who are already homeschooling often make progress fastest, because they know exactly what isn't working.

What if I'm not in Southern Maine?

All four sessions are available virtually for families anywhere. Southern Maine families can choose in-home sessions, but many of my long-term families have worked with me entirely over video call.

How is this different from a tutor or a co-op?

A tutor or co-op teaches your child. I work with you, the parent. THRIVE builds and carries the system behind your homeschool, so you can stay the primary teacher without doing all the behind-the-scenes work yourself.

My kids are very different ages. Can this work?

Yes. Mixed-age households are one of THRIVE's strengths. Each child gets their own agenda, and the household system makes sure everyone flows through the day together. And every sibling is 50% off.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

When a spot opens, I'll reach out with a short, low-pressure conversation about your family and whether THRIVE is the right fit. If it is, I'll send the contract and payment link. If it isn't, I'll tell you what will serve you better.
